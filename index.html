State Tier Key Programs JJ-Specific
North Carolina HFW Program
NC's NCDHHS Tiered Care Coordination (TCC) program launched July 2017, with HFW as Tier 3 — intensive care coordination for youth exiting JJ facilities or at risk of out-of-home placement. 16+ active HFW teams as of March 2022, targeting 30 teams by end of FY 2023. NC's detention numbers fell ~76% from peak (1999) to 2021 — among the steepest declines nationally. Teams are managed through LME-MCOs (Vaya Health, Trillium, etc.) with Family Navigators co-located at JJ and child welfare offices.

NC Tiered Care Coordination Model
NCDHHS Three-Tier Structure
Tier 1 — Universal
LME-MCO Liaisons + Family Navigators

Co-located at juvenile justice and child welfare offices. Provides access to assessors and connects youth in outpatient services. Low/no service level needs.

Tier 2 — Moderate
Family Navigator + LME-MCO Liaison

Addresses youth with moderate service needs. Outpatient with low/moderate need, or enhanced services with case management. Also includes assessor access.

Tier 3 — Intensive
High Fidelity Wraparound (HFW)

For youth exiting JJ facilities, YDCs, detention, or at risk of out-of-home placement. Team includes Care Manager, Family Peer Advocate, Youth Peer Advocate. Up to 18 months.

Managed Care Organizations delivering Tier 3 HFW across NC regions
Vaya Health

Western NC — 24 counties including Buncombe, Henderson, Haywood, Watauga, Wilkes, Ashe. Serves youth 3–17 (SED) and 18–21 (SMI) transitioning from JJ/YDC facilities.

Trillium Health Resources

Eastern NC — 27 counties including Wayne, Pitt, Craven, Johnston, Onslow, New Hanover. 2019 Network Analysis drove HFW expansion for JJ-involved youth. Max 18 months.

Alliance Health

Central NC — Durham, Wake, Cumberland, Johnston, Orange counties. Co-located navigators at Durham JJ offices (TCC Year 1 pilot site). HFW credentialed teams serving metro populations.

Cardinal Innovations

Piedmont NC — Mecklenburg, Cabarrus, Rowan, Stanly, Union, Anson. Charlotte metro flagship HFW teams. Integrated with Smart Start family support programs.
